# NovaCart Inventory Policy

## Coverage targets

- Target inventory coverage is 21 to 45 days of demand for normal products.
- High-demand products target 14 to 30 days of coverage.
- Slow-moving products should not exceed 60 days of coverage without review.

## Reorder rules

- When on-hand quantity falls to or below the reorder point, create a
  purchase requisition within 24 hours.
- Unreliable suppliers require a 25% safety-stock buffer above the
  standard reorder point.

## Stockouts

- Any SKU with two stockout events in 30 days must be escalated to
  Operations.
- Promotional demand forecasts must include a stockout-probability check
  before campaign launch.
