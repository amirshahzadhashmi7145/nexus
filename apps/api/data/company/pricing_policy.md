# NovaCart Pricing Policy

## Discount authority

- Price cuts up to 5% may be approved by category managers.
- Price cuts between 5% and 15% require Finance review.
- Price cuts above 15% require executive approval.

## Promotion rules

- Products with inventory coverage below 14 days require manager approval
  for promotional campaigns.
- High-demand products should not be discounted more than 10% without a
  stockout-risk simulation.
- Bundle discounts must preserve at least a 20% gross margin.

## Price increases

- Cost-driven increases should be communicated 7 days in advance to
  key accounts.
- Experimental A/B price tests are limited to 30 days and 10% of traffic.
