# NovaCart Finance Policy

## Margin guardrails

- Gross margin below 15% on a SKU requires Finance exception.
- Simulation-backed pricing decisions should report expected profit and
  downside (p10) profit.
- Working-capital impact of inventory builds above 45 days coverage must
  be reviewed monthly.

## Approvals

- Unbudgeted spend over $5,000 needs Finance sign-off.
- Revenue recognition follows shipment confirmation, not order creation.
