# NovaCart Marketing Policy

## Campaign gates

- Campaigns that discount inventory coverage below 14 days need manager
  approval (see pricing and inventory policies).
- Paid campaigns must name a primary KPI: revenue, new customers, or
  sell-through.
- Budget above $10,000 requires Finance co-approval.

## Messaging

- Do not promise delivery dates that Operations cannot meet for the
  targeted SKUs.
- Claims about savings must match the active price file.
