# NovaCart Supplier Policy

## Reliability tiers

- Reliable suppliers: preferred for high-demand SKUs; lead time target 3 days.
- Average suppliers: standard lead time 7 days.
- Unreliable suppliers: lead time 14 days; dual-source when share of
  category spend exceeds 30%.

## Risk controls

- Do not award more than 40% of a category to a single unreliable supplier.
- Late deliveries above 15% in a quarter trigger a supplier review meeting.
- Critical components must have at least one backup supplier on file.
