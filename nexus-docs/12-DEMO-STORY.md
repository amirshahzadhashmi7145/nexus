# Hackathon Demo Story

## Opening

> "Most business software tells you what happened. NEXUS lets you
> explore what could happen before you make the decision."

## Scenario

NovaCart is considering a 10% price reduction on a high-volume product.

The manager asks:

> "What happens if we reduce Product P-172 by 10% for the next 30 days?"

## Step 1 --- Observe

NEXUS retrieves:

-   historical sales
-   inventory
-   margin
-   supplier lead time
-   customer segments
-   pricing policy

## Step 2 --- Reason

Manager Agent creates a decision plan.

Finance Agent calculates financial constraints.

Operations Agent analyzes inventory and supplier risk.

Research Agent retrieves relevant policies.

## Step 3 --- Generate strategies

NEXUS compares:

-   no change
-   5% reduction
-   10% reduction
-   15% reduction

## Step 4 --- Simulate

NEXUS runs thousands of possible futures.

The UI displays progress.

## Step 5 --- Evaluate

The critic identifies:

-   best expected profit
-   downside risk
-   stockout probability
-   assumptions
-   uncertainty

## Step 6 --- Recommend

Example:

> "Under the current assumptions, the 10% reduction has the best
> risk-adjusted expected profit. The major risk is inventory depletion
> if demand increases faster than expected."

## Step 7 --- Human approval

The manager chooses:

**Approve**

## Step 8 --- Act

NEXUS calls the sandbox action tool.

The simulated price is updated.

## Step 9 --- Audit

The user can open the trace and inspect the complete reasoning workflow.

## Closing

> "NEXUS turns AI from a system that answers questions into a system
> that helps organizations reason about consequences."
